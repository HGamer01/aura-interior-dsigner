The Concept

Blurring the lines between indoor and outdoor living, The Glass Villa relies on vast transparent expanses and natural stone elements to connect the luxurious interior directly with the surrounding Californian landscape.

Bespoke furniture selections and gold accents provide a warm contrast to the minimalist architectural shell.
